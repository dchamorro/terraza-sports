# Payments collected in-app, not billed to the club member statement

All Charges flow through BAC Credomatic at booking time. Revenue is never posted to the club's legacy membership billing system. This is a deliberate product decision so that app-generated revenue is cleanly attributable and ROI is measurable independently of the club's existing billing. It means the app must own the full payment and refund lifecycle with no reconciliation bridge to the club's accounting system in v1.
